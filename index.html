About AnyScan209

Quick Start Guide

1. Install AnyScan209 on your Android device.

2. Open the app and create a new scan file (min 8x8, max 100 grid).

3. Use the navigation bar to move through grid cells and record data.

4. Switch between 2D and 3D modes for visualization.

5. Export your scan as OBJ, STL, or CSV for further analysis.
