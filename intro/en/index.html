🎯 Real-World Uses of AnyScan209

🌍 Ground Magnetic Scanner

The phone's magnetometer measures the Earth's magnetic field and reveals subsurface anomalies:

  • Mapping magnetic anomalies in soil
  • Detecting cavities and voids underground
  • Surveying buried structures
  • Shallow geophysical studies

🧱 Wall Scanner

Before drilling into a wall, scan it with your phone:

  • Electrical wires — only when current is flowing (with a high load)
  • Metal/iron pipes and junction boxes (not plastic)
  • Rebar and reinforcement inside concrete
  • Any metal/iron object behind plaster and tiles

🧲 Magnetic Metal Detector

Use the phone's magnetometer to detect ferromagnetic objects:

  • Lost iron objects (keys, screws, nails)
  • Shallow buried iron objects
  • Iron pipes and rebar underground
  • Hidden iron structures

⚡ DC/AC Ammeter — Direct & Alternating Current Measurement

Measure wire current using the phone's magnetometer and simple calibration — like a current transformer:

  • DC (direct current) measurement — with balancer and calibration
  • AC (alternating current) measurement — with peak-to-peak detection
  • Trace live electrical wires (heater, electric kettle)
  • Real-time monitoring with a bipolar gauge

🎨 Color Mapping with 43 Color Palettes

Visualize your data with 43 diverse color palettes:

  • 43 built-in color palettes (spectrum, thermal, natural, etc.)
  • Custom texture on maps
  • RGB channel removal, color shift, amplify
  • Crop and focus on specific regions

🔊 Acoustic Mapping

Map noise and sound in your environment:

  • Industrial noise analysis
  • Soundproofing verification
  • Acoustic environment mapping

💡 Light Mapping

Measure and map light intensity:

  • Light distribution analysis
  • Lighting design
  • Greenhouse and studio checks

⛰️ Elevation Mapping

Elevation mapping with the phone's barometer:

  • Surface topography
  • Slope and height difference analysis
  • Civil engineering projects
⚠️ Note:
The phone's internal magnetometer only detects ferromagnetic objects (iron, steel, nickel) and AC magnetic fields from live wires. Copper, aluminum, brass, gold, and plastic cannot be detected with the internal magnetometer.

✅ Solution: By connecting an external metal detector via Bluetooth (e.g., HC-05) to AnyScan209, you can scan all metals — including copper, aluminum, brass, and gold. Just send the external detector's output to the app.
🚨 SAFETY WARNING:
Never attempt to measure current in high-voltage or high-current locations such as copper busbars. Doing so puts you at serious risk of electric shock — especially if your phone has a metal body. Always follow electrical safety procedures.
