🔒 Privacy Policy for AnyScan209

Last Updated: June 25, 2026

1. Information Collection

AnyScan209 does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or sensitive user data. The application operates entirely offline and does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

2. Data Usage

The app only processes:

  • Local sensor data (magnetometer, Bluetooth) temporarily for real-time visualization.
  • User-created project files which are stored locally on the device.

No data is ever sent to external servers or third parties.

3. Required Permissions

  • Storage: To save project files and exported maps locally.
  • Bluetooth & Location: Required by Android system to discover and connect to external hardware modules.
  • Camera (if applicable): Only used for optional documentation purposes; no images are stored or shared.

4. Data Security

Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of personal data exposure. All project files remain solely on the user's device.

5. Third-Party Links

The app may contain links to download pages (e.g., Uptodown, APKPure). These external sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.
